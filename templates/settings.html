Recording
Where recordings are saved: a folder STB-Proxy can write to, such as a mounted share. Empty turns recording off. Starting a recording needs 1 GB free there, and a recording stops when less than 300 MB is left.
Plex

Jellyfin
Lets block switches and dead marks update Jellyfin's Live TV. Leave blank to turn this off.
Make one in Jellyfin under Dashboard → API Keys.
Adds STB-Proxy to Jellyfin as a tuner (the playlist) and a guide (the XMLTV), if it isn't there already. Save the address and key first.
